⚠︎ Passive RF Privacy Observatory

Nightjar is a receive-only research & awareness instrument. It demonstrates how vehicles are re-identifiable by the RF identifiers they broadcast in the clear (TPMS, BLE) and how those can be tied to a license plate.

  • Operate only on RF already broadcast in the clear, in an environment you're authorized to monitor.
  • Nightjar never transmits. It is for privacy assessment & education, not surveillance of individuals.
  • You are responsible for compliance with local law on RF interception, recording, and imagery.
